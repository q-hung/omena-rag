Business continuity

Recovery objectives
The recovery point objective (RPO) is 1 hour: in a disaster, at most the last hour of data may be lost. The recovery time objective (RTO) is 4 hours: service is restored within 4 hours of a declared disaster.

Backups
The primary database is backed up continuously. Full backups are taken daily and kept for 35 days. Backups are stored in a different region from the primary database.

Testing
Restoring from backup is tested every quarter. Results are reviewed by the engineering lead.
