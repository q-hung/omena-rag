# Data retention and deletion

## Retention periods

Account and billing records are kept for the life of the subscription plus 7 years, as required for financial records. Webhook delivery logs are kept for 30 days. Application logs are kept for 90 days.

## Deletion on request

A workspace owner can request deletion of their workspace data. Deletion from the primary database is completed within 30 days of the request. Encrypted backups are overwritten within a further 35 days.

## Deleting a workspace

When a subscription is cancelled, the workspace stays readable for 30 days so the owner can export data. After that the workspace is scheduled for deletion.
