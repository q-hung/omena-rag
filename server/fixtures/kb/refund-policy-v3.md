# Eligibility — monthly plans

On monthly plans, customers may request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. Requests after 14 days are not eligible.

# Eligibility — annual plans

On annual plans, customers may request a full refund of the annual payment within 14 days of that payment.

# Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

# Exceptions

Exceptions to these terms may be approved by Billing.
